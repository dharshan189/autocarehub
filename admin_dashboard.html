Vehicle Care & Insurance Concepts

Core Modules Explained

1. Policy Management

Storing insurance carrier name, policy number, and expiry. In this system, customers add this to their vehicle profile to enable 'Cashless' booking.

2. Surveyor Approval

When a claim is filed, the insurance company sends a Surveyor to inspect the damage assessment details. They approve the 'Approved Insurance Amount'.

3. Settlement Calculation

Final Bill = Insurance Share + Customer Deductible. The deductible is a fixed amount or percentage the customer pays regardless of the insurance coverage.

4. Cashless Claims

The customer only pays the deductible at the counter. The service center collects the rest directly from the insurance carrier after completing the work.

Project Data Flow

  • 1

    Customer books a service and checks 'Apply for Insurance Claim'.

  • 2

    Technician inspects the vehicle and enters 'Damage Assessment' details.

  • 3

    Admin reviews the assessment, assigns Surveyor details, and enters Approved Amounts.

  • 4

    Invoice Bill automatically splits the total cost between Insurance and Customer.
